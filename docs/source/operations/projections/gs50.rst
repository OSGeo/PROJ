.. _gs50:

********************************************************************************
Modified Stereographic of the 50 U.S. states
********************************************************************************

+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Classification**  | Azimuthal                                                |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Available forms** | Forward and inverse, spherical and ellipsoidal           |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Defined area**    | All 50 states of the U.S.                                |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Alias**           | gs50                                                     |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Domain**          | 2D                                                       |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Input type**      | Geodetic coordinates                                     |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+
| **Output type**     | Projected coordinates                                    |
+---------------------+----------------------------------------------------------+


.. figure:: ./images/gs50.png
   :width: 500 px
   :align: center
   :alt:   Modified Stereographic of the 50 U.S. states

   proj-string: ``+proj=gs50``

Parameters
################################################################################

.. note:: All parameters are optional for the projection.

A nonzero ellipsoid eccentricity selects the fixed ellipsoidal form, with
semi-major axis 6378206.4 m and squared eccentricity 0.00676866. A sphere selects
the spherical form with a fixed radius of 6370997 m.
